How to play

The basics

Everyone gets two private cards. Five shared cards come out in three steps: the flop (three cards), the turn (one) and the river (one). Your hand is the best five cards you can make from your two and the five shared ones. Win by having the best hand at the end, or by getting everyone else to fold.

Each round two players put in forced bets called blinds to start the pot. The white D button marks the dealer; the blinds sit to their left.

On your turn

Fold
Give up this hand. You lose what you've already bet, nothing more.
Check
Pass without betting, when there's nothing to pay.
Call
Match the current bet to stay in.
Raise
Bet more. Everyone else has to match it or fold.
All-in
Bet every chip you have.

What makes this game different

  • Whisper one private message to each player every round. AIs tell you how much it swayed them.
  • Announce one thing to the whole table every round. Every AI still in the hand reacts.
  • Each AI has a weak spot (shown in the Whisper panel), moods, and feelings about you. They see everyone's cards after each round and remember how you play.
  • Knock out enough AIs and the humans win.
